Welcome to my second lesson

I am learning how to build a website. It all started with a simple curiosity about how my favorite web pages worked behind the scenes, and that curiosity slowly turned into a genuine passion for coding. Along the way, I've discovered that HTML gives a page its structure and meaning, CSS brings it to life with color, layout, and style, and JavaScript adds interactivity that makes a website feel dynamic and alive. Every small project teaches me something new, whether it's a clever trick for centering a div, a better way to organize my code, or a fresh appreciation for accessibility and clean design. I still have a long way to go, but each lesson brings me one step closer to building the kinds of websites I've always admired, and I'm excited to keep learning, experimenting, and growing as a developer.
